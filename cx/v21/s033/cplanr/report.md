## Rotation and binding-wait amplification

`chand/report.md` measured **195,683 / 241,991 = 0.8086 source rotations/request**, or 1.2366 completions/rotation, at its recorded HEAD. It did not measure target rotations or a current-HEAD rotation ratio. Do not infer rotation count from mutex samples.

The Framework mechanisms on this HEAD are:

1. `rotate:2155` calls `dispatch_ready`; `public_host_runtime.cpp:7465` calls `begin_receive_turn`. `raw_mesh_node_owner.cpp:3334–3336` uses supplied readiness, otherwise calls `port->poll(0)`. Thus a locally continued rotation with no carried readiness enters binding wait even without blocking.
2. `rotate:2334` resets readiness. A productive rotation sets `local_pending` (`:2312`) and skips blocking wait, then the next rotation performs the zero-time poll above. Mailbox-ready and supply branches can also skip the blocking wait.
3. When no local/mailbox/supply work exists, `rotate:2339` calls `wait_for_dispatch_activity`; its deadline-composed wait reaches `wait_for_activity` and port poll. The returned optional readiness is carried into the next turn, avoiding that turn's initial zero poll when engaged. Completion/control/permit/management wakes also produce rotations; management completion can signal through `:2119–2121`.

This explains **additional entry paths**, not their sampled proportions. Source binding wait attribution is 13.884 locks + 1.289 heap/request versus binding-only 4.189 locks/request; excess **9.695 locks/request**. Neither that difference nor 0.8086 rotations identifies calls per wait, zero-time/blocking proportions, empty wakes, or an avoidable fraction. Completion draining, registration observation and native locking remain binding concerns. No part of that 9.695 is credited as a saving here. Carrying POLLIN forever, suppressing wakes, delaying management, adding a ready flag or introducing a second poller is outside the allowed plan.
